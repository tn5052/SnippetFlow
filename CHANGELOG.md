## [1.0] - 2026-10-21

### Added

- Initial public release.
- Region-based text capture with on-device OCR.
- QR code and barcode capture.
- Image import for OCR.
- Line-break controls and optional clipboard history.
- Text-to-speech support.
- Custom words list and language selection.
- Configurable global shortcuts.
- Launch-at-login and menu-bar visibility settings.
