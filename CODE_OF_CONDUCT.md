# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SnippetFlow to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience, background, identity, or ability. Contributors and maintainers pledge
to make participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept actionable technical feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information,
  clipboard contents, screenshots, or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Threats, doxxing, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other communication associated with the project. It also applies when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
maintainer through the project's private reporting channel:

<https://github.com/tn5052/SnippetFlow/security/advisories/new>

Please include a description of what happened and any relevant links. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. The maintainer will respect
the privacy and safety of the reporter and will avoid sharing identifying details
unless necessary to address the report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code. Responses may include a warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces, depending on
the severity and pattern of behavior.
